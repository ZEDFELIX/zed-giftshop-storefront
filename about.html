Our story

Nairobi's gift specialists

ZED Gift Shop began in 2014 with one wrapping table in Westlands and a simple idea: a gift should arrive beautiful, on time, and with a story worth telling. Today we design, personalize and deliver gifts across Kenya from our Nairobi workshop.

How your order flows

1. You orderChoose your gift, add personalization and pay with M-Pesa or card.
2. We personalizeEngraving, printing and wrapping are done in-house and checked by hand.
3. We pack and dispatchYour parcel is labelled and handed to ZED Express the same day.
4. DeliveredTracked in real time, with free wrapping and a card included.

Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to fulfil your order: your name, delivery address, phone number, email and what you bought. Payment details are handled by our payment providers — we never see or store your M-Pesa PIN or full card number. We do not sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing hello@zedgiftshop.co.ke.

Terms & conditions

Prices are in Kenyan shillings and include VAT. Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees — we do our best to meet them.
